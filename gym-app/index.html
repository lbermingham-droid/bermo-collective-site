For the coach

Run the floor from one screen.

Built around how a class gym actually works: you plan by the month, you never know who will walk in, and you have about ten seconds per person.

01 · Plan the month

Every Monday is Lower A. Done.

Set the workout for each weekday once and fill the whole month. Change a single day when the plan changes. The calendar shows what ran and how many came.

02 · Check the class in

Tick who came. Type what they lifted.

The morning's class is one sheet. Tick a member and today's lifts appear on their row, with what they lifted last time already showing. No clipboard, no retyping later.

03 · Know your members

Who is showing up. Who is slipping.

Visits, last visit, volume and PRs for every member, by day, week, month, year or any dates you pick. The answer to "how is she doing?" takes one tap, not a binder.

For your members

The reason they keep coming back.

Members stay where they can see themselves getting stronger. This puts the proof in their pocket, under your gym's name.

01 · Every lift, every PR

Their numbers, by lift, by date.

Each lift shows the heaviest set, the best session and every day they did it. A new PR is marked the day it happens.

02 · What today worked

The muscles light up.

After class, the figure shows exactly which muscles that workout hit and which it missed, with one lift to add for the gap.

03 · Their week at a glance

This week's classes, ticked off.

Home shows the week, what is done and what is next, plus food and water if they want to track it. Nothing to learn.

What is fractional brand growth?

Instead of hiring a full-time marketing executive or paying agency retainers, you get a strategic growth partner who steps inside your business part-time. We handle strategy and execution together, moving the right things forward without the overhead.

How is this different from hiring an agency?

Agencies hand you a deliverable and move on. We embed into your business, clarify priorities, and actually execute alongside you. There's no handoff, no waiting for revisions. We move at the speed your business needs.

Who is BERMO.COLLECTIVE for?

Founder-led businesses, startups, lifestyle brands, and small businesses that are ready to grow but stuck on where to start. If you know something isn't working but can't pinpoint the break, that's exactly where we come in.

What does the free brand audit include?

A 10-question diagnostic that takes about 2 minutes. You'll get a clear snapshot of where your business is breaking and what to prioritize first. No strings attached.

How quickly do you start seeing results?

Momentum builds from day one. We don't do ramp-up theater. Within the first week, we're identifying priorities and moving on the things that matter most. Most clients see measurable shifts within 30 days.

Can't I just use AI to do this myself?

AI doesn't fix confusion. It speeds it up. Without clarity, you get more content, more noise, and a brand that starts to sound like everyone else. AI should amplify you, not replace you. This is AI leveraged, human led.

What is the ROI? I can't afford strategy.

You can't afford not to fix what's slowing you down. A slow quarter, missed launch, or underperforming funnel can easily cost $20K to $100K+ in lost revenue. This isn't strategy for the sake of it — this is identifying exactly where growth is leaking and fixing it fast.

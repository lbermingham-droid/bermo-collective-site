The Mascot Philosophy

The Salesforce Rule. Applied to BERMO.

Salesforce uses Astro, Codey, Cloudy, and Einstein to make complex enterprise software feel human. BERMO uses Kiera and BB to make a complex execution system feel personal and trustworthy. The mascots do not explain the tech. They carry the emotion.

Rule 01

Never a cartoon character

Write Kiera and BB as brand personas. Not mascots. Not cartoon characters. Kiera is a quality guardian. BB is an alignment operator. They represent infrastructure.

Rule 02

Placement is meaning

Kiera always appears near trust and protection elements: vetting, quality, The Shop, the gap. BB always appears near logic and continuity: loading, diagnostics, workflows, the network.

Rule 03

Together only with intent

Kiera and BB share the frame only when the story requires both: the discovery AND the fix. Kiera finds the crack. BB scans and loads. Do not mix them casually.

Usage Guidelines

The Rules of Deployment

Scale and Clear Space

Minimum Size
Kiera: minimum 120px wide in any digital context. BB: minimum 60px diameter. Below these sizes details are lost.
Clear Space
Always maintain at least 1x the mascot's height as clear space on all sides. Never crowd the mascot with heavy text or other graphic elements.
Color Pairings
Kiera on cyan backgrounds: use the crouching hunting pose. Kiera on black backgrounds: use the standing upright pose. BB on black or very dark backgrounds only for full visual impact.

Animation Guidelines

Kiera in Motion
Sharp. Purposeful. No bouncing. Movement is directional, deliberate. Like a predator tracking something. Ease-in-out curves, no spring physics.
BB in Motion
Smooth. Looping. Gentle float or bob. The loading bar fills with a glow trail. Eyes blink occasionally. Spring physics are acceptable for BB.
Together in Motion
Only animate both when showing the full story: Kiera finds the crack, BB scans, The Solution loads. Three-beat narrative. Never casual side-by-side animation.

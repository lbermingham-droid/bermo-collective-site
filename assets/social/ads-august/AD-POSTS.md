# HOW TO RUN THESE (the mechanics matter as much as the copy)

1. **Post from Lexi's personal LinkedIn profile.** Not the company page. This is worth more than everything else on this list combined (5–8x engagement difference). Repost from the company page an hour later if you want — the personal post is the ad.
2. **Post when you can stay for 60 minutes.** The first hour decides reach: 3+ real comment threads in hour one ≈ 5x amplification. Reply to every single comment — your replies count too.
3. **Deliver every keyword comment by DM, fast.** The DM is the sale. Open with their name, deliver the thing, ask ONE question ("what kind of business is it?"). No pitch in message one.
4. **On Instagram, wire the keywords to auto-DM** (ManyChat or any Meta-API tool — ToS-safe). Comment-to-DM flows convert 12–18% vs 2–3% for "link in bio."
5. **Cadence:** one per week, in this order: 2 (FOUND — strongest hook), 1 (SITE), 4 (HOURS), 3 (WINDOW). Post 2's Reel can also run as a LinkedIn native video later — don't burn both versions the same week.
6. **Never put bermoco.com in the post body.** First comment only. The 40–50% link penalty is real.
7. **Reel specs (Post 2):** 7–15 seconds total, on-screen captions throughout, no intro — the question is on screen by second 1. Kill any take where the point lands after second 3.
8. **These are written to sound like a person on purpose.** Don't let any tool "polish" them — AI-sounding posts earn 57% less engagement (1.2M-post study). The slightly-too-plain sentences are the strategy.

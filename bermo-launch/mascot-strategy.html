Meet Kiera & BB.

Two characters. Two jobs. One brand. Inspired by the Salesforce playbook (Astro, Codey, Cloudy, Blaze), Kiera and BB are not decoration. They are product roles with a face.

Character profiles

Two personas. Defined.

Kiera the Wolf

Hunter. Protector. The quality guardian.

Sharp instincts. Quiet confidence. Does not announce herself. Results do. Kiera shows up first. She finds what is broken before anyone else names it.

  • Tied to: The Alignment Review, The Shop discovery phase, the audit
  • Voice: Declarative. Short. Sure. No softening. No filler.
  • Use for: Audit announcements, conference materials, "we see what others miss" content
  • Avoid for: Warm community moments, celebration posts, anything requiring softness
BB the Bot

Always there. Human led. The period behind the brand.

Steady. Curious. Accessible. The human in the machine. BB runs the handoffs and infrastructure so specialists, pods, and voice stay in sync.

  • Tied to: The Fleet, the always-on command center, trust and continuity
  • Voice: Conversational question. Warm. A little nerdy. Helpful by default.
  • Use for: Onboarding, waitlist confirmations, social proof, "always-on" content
  • Avoid for: Heavy sales moments, pure authority positioning

Usage guidelines

Rules for scale, color, and tone.

Scale

Minimum & clear space

Minimum height: 80px. Always leave half the mascot's height as clear space on all sides. Never crop the silhouette.

Color pairings

Where each lives

Kiera lives on cyan or cream. BB lives on near-black or cyan. Never put either on pink, red, or any color outside the brand palette.

Animation

Movement personality

Kiera: sharp, purposeful, single-direction motion. BB: smooth, looping micro-animations (blink, hover-bob, pulse).

Voice

How they would caption

Kiera writes a statement. BB asks a question. Never put both voices in the same caption.

Together

Pairing rules

Only put both mascots in the same frame when there is a narrative reason: a discovery moment, a deployment moment, a trust handoff.

Do not

Hard nos

Do not write about either as a cartoon character. Do not redesign their outfits. Do not put words in their speech bubbles that the founder would not say.

Print specs

Send to a printer.

Standard 3.5 × 2 inch with 0.125 inch bleed on all sides (3.75 × 2.25 trim). 300 dpi. Both sides print full bleed. Round corners optional.

Step 1

Open in Chrome.

Or any modern browser. Avoid Safari for printing — it scales unpredictably.

Step 2

File → Print.

Or ⌘ P (Mac) / Ctrl P (Windows). Select "Save as PDF" as the destination.

Step 3

Margins: None. Scale: 100%.

Background graphics: ON. Save the PDF. Send to MOO, Vistaprint, or your local printer.

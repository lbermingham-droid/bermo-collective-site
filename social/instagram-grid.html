Production Workflow

Batch in Canva.
Under One Hour.

1

Create two base templates in Canva

Template A: 1080x1080, near-black background (#0a0a0a), white Montserrat 900 headline, small cyan label. Template B: 1080x1080, cream background (#f0efe9), black Montserrat 900 headline, dark label.

2

Open Apps and search Bulk Create

In the left menu, go to Apps, search for Bulk Create, and click it. Choose Enter data manually. A table opens.

3

Paste the tile headlines into the table

Column 1: Tile label (e.g. "Kiera"). Column 2: Headline text (e.g. "Five minutes. That is all she needs."). Use the copy above from each tile's design note.

4

Connect data to your text boxes

Right-click each text element in your template, select Connect Data, and choose the column. Canva links your data to the design.

5

Generate and export all tiles

Click Generate Pages. Canva creates all variations instantly. Export as PNG 1080x1080. Drop mascot images onto the relevant tiles. Schedule in Buffer or Later.

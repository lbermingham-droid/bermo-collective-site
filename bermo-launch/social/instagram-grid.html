The reel

One take. Phone. Window light.

The whole point is no makeup stress, no clean-house stress, no editing rabbit hole. The rawness is the credibility signal.

Hook · 2 seconds

"I walk into a brand and within five minutes I know."

On-screen text in BERMO display font. Show before the founder speaks.

Body · 30 to 45 seconds

Talking head, one take.

Founder speaks directly to camera. Three short punchy lines. One question. CTA.

B-roll

None required.

No screen recordings, no overlays, no transitions. The face is the shot.

Hook + 3 lines + question + CTA.

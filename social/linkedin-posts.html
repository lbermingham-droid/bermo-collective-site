LinkedIn Strategy

How to Build a Following Before You Launch

The Hook

Every post starts with a question or a short punchy declaration. "When did you stop being a founder?" stops the scroll. That is the entire job of line one.

The Story

Short paragraphs. Never more than 3 lines. LinkedIn surfaces the "See more" break around line 3-4. Make those first lines earn the click.

The Ask

Every post ends with a question directed at the reader. "What is stalling your growth?" gets replies. Replies tell the algorithm this is worth showing to more people.

Video Post

60 Seconds. One Take.
Maximum Credibility.

Setup
  • Window light, not ring light
  • Phone propped or held — either works
  • Natural background — no need to clean
  • No makeup requirement
  • One take. The rawness is the point.
Why It Works

LinkedIn rewards authenticity. Over-produced content reads as an ad. Talking head videos with direct eye contact perform better than polished studio cuts because they trigger conversational engagement. The founder who looks real earns more trust than the one who looks like a commercial.

          Paste your Anthropic or OpenAI API key to unlock photo + text food logging. Cost: ~$0.005 per photo (Claude) or ~$0.005 (GPT-4o-mini). Get a key at console.anthropic.com or platform.openai.com.

          Privacy: The key is stored ONLY in this browser (localStorage). Photos and text are sent directly from your browser to the AI provider for parsing. Set a spend limit on your account to cap monthly cost.

          Reminders + accountability

          Three nudges built for "get your ass to the gym" / "drink water" / "don't end the day with nothing tracked." No meal-time pings.

          1. In-app banners show on the dashboard the moment you open the app and a trigger fires. Always on if enabled, no permissions needed.
          2. Browser notifications are best-effort — they fire while the app is open or recently in the background. Without a backend they can't wake your phone after hours of being closed. Add to Home Screen on iPhone for the best chance.

          Apple Health sync (workaround)

          Web apps can't read Apple Health directly. Use the iOS Shortcut below — it exports today's weight, steps, sleep, and active energy to a JSON file in iCloud Drive. Then tap "Import" to pull it in.

          1. Open Shortcuts on iPhone → tap + → name it "BERMO Health Export".
          2. Add actions: Find Health Samples (Weight, Steps, Sleep, Active Energy — today) → Combine Text as JSON → Save File to iCloud Drive folder BERMO as health-today.json.
          3. Run it once a day (or set an Automation at 11pm).
          4. Come back here and tap Import latest export.
